Notion 2.0 - Re-imagining Notion with digital teammates

TL;DR

  • Rebuilt Notion from scratch with AI agents embedded directly in the editor - no more tab switching or workflow fragmentation.
  • Four digital teammates live inside your docs: Search Agent (inline Google results), Image Agent (infographics + personalized photos), Video Agent (summarize, transcribe, clip videos), and General List Agent (process spreadsheets with natural language).
  • Demonstrated with a NYC trip planning use case: weather lookup, custom infographics, video summaries, Google Sheets filtering, and video clipping - all without leaving the doc.
  • Page Merging feature intelligently combines multiple pages by finding overlaps and suggesting a unified structure.

The Problem

Every time I plan something in Notion, I end up with 15 browser tabs open. Search for weather? New tab. Create an infographic? Switch to Canva. Summarize a video? Open ChatGPT. Extract data from a spreadsheet? Back to Google Sheets.

The irony is that AI tools are everywhere now, but they all live outside your actual workspace. You're constantly context-switching, copying and pasting, breaking your flow.

The Vision

What if AI teammates lived inside your documents? Not as a sidebar chatbot, but as collaborators that work directly in your content. You highlight text, trigger an agent, and the result appears right where you need it.

So I rebuilt Notion from scratch using Claude Code - keeping all the familiar building blocks (pages, slash commands, drag-and-drop blocks) but adding four specialized digital teammates.

The Digital Teammates

Search Agent

Highlight any text, hit Cmd+2 (or click the search icon), and your text turns green - that's your search query. The agent runs a Google search and embeds the results inline, right in your document. No tab switching, no copy-paste.

Image Agent

Turn any text block into a beautiful infographic using Nano Banana under the hood. Or toggle "use my face" to generate personalized photos - like me ice skating in Central Park for a trip itinerary. Context engineering makes them Instagram-ready.

Video Agent

Embed a YouTube video, then ask the agent to summarize it, list key points, or generate a full transcript. The magic: select any excerpt from the transcript with a purple cursor, and it clips that exact segment and saves it to your Google Drive.

General List Agent

Embed a Google Sheet, then query it with natural language. "Pull the restaurant names ranked 1, 2, 3" - the agent understands the spreadsheet structure, identifies the right columns, and extracts exactly what you need.

Page Merging

When you have multiple pages scattered around a project, drag and drop them together. The agent reads through all content, finds overlaps, identifies where information can be consolidated, and suggests a merged, well-formatted result.

The Shift

This isn't about replacing Notion - it's about reimagining what a workspace looks like when AI is a first-class citizen. The goal: one integrated environment where you think, your teammates execute, and the results appear exactly where you need them.

No more juggling tools. No more breaking flow. Just you and your digital teammates, building together.
