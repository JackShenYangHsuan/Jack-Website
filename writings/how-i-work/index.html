How I Work

November 2025

Bias towards actions. I understand the system first, then move fast with a high bar. Even if I'm unsure what to do, I will just do anything because action produces information.

Sequences of sprints. I go extremely hard (100hrs/wk at McKinsey), then rest on vacations.

Let the best ideas win. Allergic to politics and sloppy thinking.

Prefer shipping over perfect planning. I would rather ship a smaller, real version and iterate than wait for an "optimal" strategy on slides.

Ruthlessly truth seeking. Strong opinions, loosely held. I am fine changing my mind when the data or reality contradicts my view.
