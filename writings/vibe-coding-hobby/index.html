Vibe Coding as a Hobby

December 2025

I realized something funny recently: vibe coding has become a hobby for me.

I don't do it to start a business or make money. I do it because it's fun. It is a form of self-expression. The same way some people pick up a guitar or go for a run, I open my terminal and start building small things that make me delightful.

Tennis is my way of flexing my body and dealing with pressure. Vibe coding is my way of flexing my creative muscle. No stakes. No goals. Just curiosity and play.

And that makes it even more enjoyable.
