You Make Your Own Luck

December 2025

You make your own luck.

Luck doesn't drop out of the sky. You raise the odds by increasing the surface area where something good can hit you. And that only happens when you do things most people don't do.

When I started vibe coding and posting my work, my life changed. People I never thought I'd meet suddenly showed up. None of that would have happened if I waited for the "right moment" or a perfect plan.

Action creates information. Information shows you the next step. Sitting still gives you nothing. So even when you don't know what to do, do something. Anything. That's how luck finds you.
